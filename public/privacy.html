Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 25, 2026

B-CONNECT (Barangay & Municipal Multi-Agency Incident Reporting and Case Tracking Portal for the Municipality of Roxas, Oriental Mindoro) strictly complies with Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012) and Republic Act No. 7160 (Local Government Code).

1. Information We Collect

  • Google Sign-In Account Data: When you choose to sign in with Google via Supabase Authentication, we receive only your basic profile information—specifically your name, email address, and profile avatar URL—used solely to authenticate and provision your B-CONNECT resident account.
  • Resident & Incident Report Data: Your selected barangay, submitted incident descriptions, locations, and any supporting photos or attachments you voluntarily provide.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To verify your identity and maintain your citizen portal session.
  • To route, track, and coordinate barangay, MDRRMO, and LGU emergency or civic responses.
  • To enforce barangay-level role isolation so confidential blotter entries and witness identities are accessible only to authorized responders handling your case.

3. Data Isolation & Sharing

Your personal profile and submitted reports are isolated to your account and accessible only to authorized Barangay, MDRRMO, and LGU Roxas personnel assigned to handle your case. B-CONNECT does not sell, rent, or disclose your personal data to third-party advertisers or unrelated external entities.

4. Data Retention & Your Rights

Under RA 10173, you have the right to be informed, access, correct, or request removal of your personal account information where permitted by municipal record-keeping regulations.

5. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, please coordinate with the Municipality of Roxas LGU or MDRRMO office through the B-CONNECT portal.
